Electrical Substation Design & Protection Relay Modeling

AutoCAD MATLAB Protective Relaying Power Systems CT Saturation

Project Details

  • Designed a comprehensive three-bus (230 kV, 115 kV, 69 kV) electrical substation one-line diagram (OLD) in AutoCAD, integrating a 225 MVA auto-transformer and an 85 MVA wye-delta transformer.
  • Calculated three-phase full-load currents and analyzed potential fault conditions to accurately size and select multi-ratio Current Transformers (CTs) and Potential Transformers (PTs) for protective relaying.
  • Analyze magnetic core saturation during severe fault conditions, factoring in CT winding resistance, lead wire resistance, and relay burden to ensure secondary voltages remained within specified ANSI accuracy classes.
  • Implemented a complete protective relaying scheme using standard ANSI/IEEE C37.2 device numbers—including 87T differential, 50/51 overcurrent, and 21 distance relays—into the final AutoCAD layouts.
  • Simulated transmission line fault conditions (Single Line to Ground, Line to Line, 3-Phase) within MATLAB/Simulink to validate system response behaviors.

System Protection & Substation Drafting

This project provided a deep dive into utility-scale power protection and substation drafting. By combining fault and saturation analysis with AutoCAD drafting, I was able to bridge the gap between theoretical system calculations and practical physical layouts. Determining the precise points where CTs saturate under asymmetrical fault conditions ensured that the specified protective relays would operate reliably, safeguarding the high-value transformer assets from catastrophic failure.
