230/115/69 kV Substation Design, Protective Relaying & Real-Time Fault Analysis

AutoCAD (OLD) SEL-421L & SEL-487 ANSI/IEEE C37.2 CT Saturation (MATLAB) OPAL-RT / RT-LAB MATLAB/Simulink Tektronix DPO 2014B

Project Overview & Technical Highlights

  • Three-Bus Substation One-Line Diagram (AutoCAD DWG 100-12-1010): Engineered a complete 230 kV North Bus, 69 kV East Bus (distribution), and 115 kV West Bus (transmission) substation incorporating a wave trap, an 85 MVA 230/69 kV Wye-Delta transformer (12% impedance), and a 225 MVA 230/115 kV auto-transformer (18% impedance).
  • Load Flow & Instrument Transformer Sizing: Calculated three-phase full-load currents across all branches (778.2 A total incoming at North Bus; 213.4 A / 711.2 A on the 85 MVA unit; 564.8 A / 1129.6 A on the 225 MVA unit) and specified phase-to-neutral Potential Transformers (PT-01: 133 kV/115 V, PT-02: 40 kV/115 V, PT-03: 67 kV/115 V) alongside 3000/5 Multi-Ratio (MR) C800 bushing CTs.
  • CT Core Saturation Analysis (MATLAB): Evaluated magnetic core saturation under worst-case bolted short-circuit conditions using X/R = 6.67, 1.07 Ω relay burden, and 150 ft of 10-AWG lead wire (1.2 Ω/1000 ft) to enforce the ANSI accuracy limit (VANSI ≥ Ifault,sec × (1 + X/R) × ZBurden). Upsized low-side CT taps on breakers 6K101 (800:5 → 2000:5) and 6K102 (1200:5 → 3000:5) to prevent saturation at 5,926.90 A and 6,275.55 A fault currents.
  • Microprocessor Relay Protection Scheme: Integrated SEL-487 transformer differential protection (87T) across inner bushing CTs and SEL-421L line/backup relays implementing 50/51/67 (overcurrent/directional), 21 (distance), 27/59 (under/over-voltage), 25 (synchronism check), and 86BF (breaker failure lockout) tripping schemes across breakers 8K11, 6K101, 8K12, and 6K102.
  • OPAL-RT Real-Time HIL Fault Simulation: Modeled a 735 kV / 230 kV / 13.8 kV transmission network (6×350 MVA synchronous generator, two 180 km distributed RLC lines, 50 μs discrete step) in MATLAB/Simulink and RT-LAB, capturing real-time SLG, LL, LLG, and 3-Phase fault waveforms on dual Tektronix DPO 2014B digital phosphor oscilloscopes.

Governing Equations & Protection Calculations

The following analytical equations were implemented in MATLAB (CT-Saturation.m) to calculate full-load currents, prospective short-circuit fault levels, secondary loop burden, and ANSI core-saturation thresholds:

1. Three-Phase Full-Load Current
Irated = Srated√3 × VLL
Determines steady-state primary and secondary currents for initial CT tap sizing.
2. Prospective Short-Circuit Power
Sfault = Srated%X
Approximates bolted fault power using transformer impedance (Xfmr = 12%, XAuto = 18%).
3. CT Internal Winding Resistance
RCT = CT Burden × Desired CTRMax CT Ratio
Scales internal resistance for 3000:5 MR C800 CTs (8 Ω full-winding burden).
4. Total Secondary Burden Impedance
ZBurden = RCT + Rlead + RRelay
Combines CT resistance, 150 ft 10-AWG round-trip lead wire (1.2 Ω/1000 ft), and 1.07 Ω relay burden.
5. ANSI CT Saturation Condition
VANSI ≥ Ifault,sec × (1 + XR ) × ZBurden
Ensures the required secondary voltage stays below the C800 accuracy-class rating (VANSI = 800 V) with line X/R = 6.67, preventing magnetic core saturation during asymmetrical faults.

Engineering Takeaways

Combining AutoCAD substation drafting, MATLAB CT saturation modeling, and OPAL-RT real-time fault simulation bridged the gap between steady-state equipment sizing and dynamic grid protection. Upsizing the 69 kV and 115 kV CT taps from 800:5 and 1200:5 to 2000:5 and 3000:5 ensured that the SEL-487 differential and SEL-421L backup relays receive accurate secondary waveforms during 6+ kA faults, preventing false restraint or delayed tripping on high-value power transformers.
