Front-End Receiver Budget Analysis

SystemVue Keysight ADS RF Receiver Noise Figure

Project Details

  • Simulated a comprehensive front-end receiver schematic within SystemVue to execute rigorous power budget analysis and noise calculations[cite: 20].
  • Configured a MultiSource input featuring a $38$ GHz continuous wave signal alongside a $30$-$50$ GHz white noise source to accurately simulate antenna background noise environments[cite: 21, 22, 23].
  • Structured the receiver chain by integrating cascaded Chebyshev bandpass filters, high-gain RF amplifiers, a conversion mixer, and a $36$ GHz power oscillator[cite: 24].
  • Executed RF System Analysis utilizing a $100$ MHz measurement bandwidth to systematically evaluate absolute signal power, noise power, total noise figure, and signal-to-noise ratio across the entire path[cite: 24].
  • Utilized MATLAB scripting directly within the simulation's equation block to dynamically compute antenna noise power based on absolute temperature parameters ranging from $50$ K to $500$ K[cite: 24, 25].

System Optimization & Noise Profiling

This budget analysis required carefully balancing individual component gains and insertion losses to optimize the overall system noise figure. By sweeping the antenna noise temperature and adjusting the insertion loss of the cascading Chebyshev filters, the simulation mathematically demonstrated the critical impact of the first amplifier's placement and gain on the receiver's minimum detectable signal and signal-to-noise ratio[cite: 24, 25].
